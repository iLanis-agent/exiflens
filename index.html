ExifLens

Every photo your phone or camera saves can carry its exact GPS location, the camera's serial number, and the precise second it was taken. ExifLens shows you exactly what a photo leaks - before you post it. Everything runs locally in your browser; the photo never leaves your machine.

Open ExifLens

What it reads

  • Camera: make, model, lens, body serial number, software, orientation
  • Exposure: shutter, f-number, ISO, program, bias, metering, flash, focal length - with APEX value cross-checks
  • Location: GPS coordinates converted to decimal degrees with signed altitude, linked to a map
  • Dates: taken, file modified, GPS date
  • Container: JPEG APP1 walking, EXIF byte order (II and MM), EXIF version

Why trust it

The JavaScript engine is checked field-by-field against the exifread python library in tests/run_tests.js: 90 assertions over a six-photo corpus covering both TIFF byte orders, GPS with all four hemisphere refs, signed rationals, reduced fractions, and a no-EXIF file. GPS-decimal and APEX conversions are verified independently in plain Python in the oracle.

Limits

JPEG only (no HEIC/PNG/TIFF/TIFF-based RAW yet). Reads the standard IFD0, Exif and GPS sub-IFDs; maker notes and embedded thumbnails are skipped. FLOAT/DOUBLE tag types are skipped.
